# SEO for Instagram

Instagram search matches the **name field, the handle, and the words in captions and alt text**. Hashtags are labels, not reach: Instagram capped them at 5 in December 2025, and Mosseri said in 2025 that they don't drive distribution. The rule is to write the phrase a buyer types as a normal sentence in caption lines 1-3, and add 3 specific hashtags.

## Keywords

**Name field (pick one, see file 01):** `Black Diamond Engagement Rings`. Plain letters, never Unicode bold.

**Caption phrases, ranked by fit to what we sell:**
1. black diamond engagement ring(s)
2. black diamond ring
3. unique engagement ring / alternative engagement ring
4. natural black diamond (we use natural stones; don't drift into "salt and pepper", which is a different stone)
5. black and white diamond ring / black and gold
6. emerald cut / pear / oval / marquise black diamond (the shape of the piece in the post)
7. made to order engagement ring / handmade engagement ring
8. wedding band to match / bridal set
9. ring stack (for the many-jewels posts)

**Where they go:** one phrase in the first 125 characters, a second in the body, written the way a person would say it. Alt text for every image or carousel, e.g. "Five natural black diamond engagement rings in yellow and rose gold on two hands." Run the ig-caption linter with `--keywords` before each post.

**Never in captions:** "AI", "real diamond" (banned 20.10), delivery dates, prices in Hebrew posts, "dark" messaging about the viewer.

## 3 hashtags per post type

| Post type | Hashtags |
|---|---|
| Many-jewels reel (Concepts 1, 3) | #ringstack #blackdiamondring #uniqueengagementring |
| Single ring, product forward | #blackdiamondengagementring #[shape]engagementring (e.g. #emeraldcutring, #pearengagementring) #finejewelry |
| "Pick one" carousel (Concept 2) | #engagementringinspo #blackdiamonds #alternativebride |
| Engagement ring + band pair | #bridalset #weddingband #blackdiamondring |
| REMNANT / collection story post | #blackdiamondjewelry #handmadejewelry #finejewelry |
| Ready to ship / one of each | #readytoship #blackdiamondring #engagementring |
| Educational (stories only, then a highlight) | none in stories. If it ever becomes a carousel: #engagementringtips #ringshopping #diamondeducation |

Rotate within the type. Never more than 5 (the platform cap), never #viral, #explorepage or #fyp.
